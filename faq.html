> Q2: What exactly is "Metroid: Samus Aran"?

A: Metroid: Samus Aran is a Fan-made Web Comic based on the Nintendo Property and Character of the same name.


Simply and to the point, this comic was made as both a test to myself to see if I can handle larger art/writing projects, and as a means to see how well I can tell a limited, serialized story while working in the constraints of a pre-existing property's canon. It only sounds insane because it is.

Q3: What can be expected from this WebComic, minus spoilers?

A: A "Sci-Fi Superhero" story set in a Space Western setting.


It's a bit complicated to get into especially while avoiding major spoilers, but essentially, this story is told from the perspective of someone who, until their call to action as the story protagonist, basically has no stakes in the greater plot of the universe. it isn't until trouble comes to their door, that they try and fail to meet the problem head on, in which case they come into possession of an immense power that not only lets them take care of their problems, but unknowingly acts as a beacon for more than they could have ever imagined for themself; good and bad.
