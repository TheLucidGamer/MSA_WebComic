> Q2: What exactly is "Metroid: Samus Aran"?

A: Metroid: Samus Aran is a Fan-made Web Comic based on the Nintendo Property and Character of the same name.


Simply and to the point, this comic was made as both a test to myself to see if I can handle larger art/writing projects, and as a means to see how well I can tell a limited, serialized story while working in the constraints of a pre-existing property's canon.
